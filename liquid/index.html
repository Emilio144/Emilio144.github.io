Every part I've shipped was argued before it was cut.

Against the alloy, against the shop, against the vibration table. The drawing is where the argument happens — fully defined sketches, GD&T that means something, fasteners chosen for the thin gauge they actually sit in. Then the part goes out, and the part gets the last word.
