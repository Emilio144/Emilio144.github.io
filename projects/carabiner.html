§ 01 / design

Designed for the mold from line one.

The carabiner had to look right beside the rest of the product line and be cheap enough to mold at small-shop volumes. I modeled it in SolidWorks with draft, wall thickness and gate placement in mind from the start, so the mold vendor could quote it without a redesign.

Mold development & manufacturing

  • Prepared the CAD and drawings for mold quoting and iterated with the vendor on moldability feedback.
  • Reviewed first-article samples for fit, finish and gate function, and fed corrections back into the tool.
  • Oversaw the production run and the launch listing.

Why it matters

Most student CAD stops at a render. This one went into a mold, was made in quantity, and sold. It taught me what DFM really costs when you're the one paying for the tooling.
