Every link and joint housing is my own SolidWorks design, built around Feetech STS3215 serial-bus servos that report position. A hand-moved leader arm drives it in real time, and those sessions become training data for a LeRobot imitation-learning policy.

Most student CAD stops at a render. Mine keeps going.

Into a mold that a vendor quoted. Into a PDM vault a sheet-metal shop pulls from. Into a bus of servos that has to agree with a policy trained on my own demonstrations. The render is where the work starts.

// how this page is drawn
Every object above is one fragment shader.
Each pixel marches a ray through a signed
distance field: capsules, cylinders, boxes
and a pear-shaped loop, blended with a
polynomial smooth-min. The liquid is six
droplets and your cursor. The morph is just
mix(shape, liquid, t).

// what that means
The figures are live sketches, not CAD renders.
The arm's IK, the CNC's feed rate and the
gate's spring are simulated every frame.
